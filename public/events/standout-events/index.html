  • Spancraft

    SpanCraft is a bridge-building challenge where participants must design and construct a cable-stayed bridge along with its foundation under fixed material and budget constraints. Each team will receive a fixed initial budget to purchase foundation materials. Sand will be cheaper, while soil will be costlier but provide better foundation performance, forcing teams to make strategic decisions between cost and stability. For the bridge, teams will be provided with long wooden members and other specified materials. The completed bridge must satisfy the given dimensional requirements and will then be tested for its maximum load-bearing capacity. The winner will be the team that achieves the best combination of load capacity, dimensional accuracy and cost efficiency.

  • Roadies: The Food Saga 4.0

    In Innovision 2026, our club FTS will be hosting an event titled Roadies: The Food Saga 4.0. The event will consist of three exciting rounds designed to showcase our passion for Food Technology while blending it with fun and creativity. Round-1 In the first round, participants will be grouped as per the set limit and provided with a Dalgona cookie. They will be required to carve it into a specific shape within a given time limit. Round-2 Participants who fail to complete the task will be eliminated and will not qualify for the second round, which will feature a rapid-fire quiz on topics related to food safety, nutrition, and technology. Round-3 The final round will feature blindfolded participants who must identify different food items using their senses. This round highlights the importance of sensory analysis in making food more appealing to consumers.
