  • Dead Man's Switch

    Each team consists of 2 or 3 participants. At the beginning of the event, the team will be randomly given a physical challenge. The selected participant will be designated as the “Dead Man” and must continuously perform the assigned physical activity while the remaining team members attempt to complete two consecutive challenges. The two stages are: 1. Puzzle Challenge 2. Buzz Wire Challenge After successfully completing both stages, the team will receive two clues. Using these clues, the Dead Man must identify and answer the final question within a maximum of three attempts. The team that completes the entire challenge in the minimum time will be declared the winner. Each team must complete the entire challenge within 150 seconds ( 2 minutes and 30 seconds).

  • Circuit Heist

    Circuit Heist is a two-day, team-based technical challenge where participants have to recover a sabotaged electronic system. Instead of solving five independent questions, they progress through a connected chain of challenges. They first decode a digital clue, then debug an electronic circuit, solve a logic lock, analyze a timing waveform and finally complete a Verilog RTL challenge. Every successful stage gives them a key to access the next one. Hints and wrong submissions carry time penalties, so both technical ability and strategy matter. The final recovered keys lead to a system-recovery code, and teams are ranked based on their effective completion time.

  • REACT

    REACT: The IIChE Engineering Challenge is an interactive, team-based chemical engineering competition that goes beyond the conventional quiz format by combining technical knowledge, problem-solving, strategy, and resource management. Participants will be provided with a limited set of stationery, hint tokens, process cards, and other resources, which they must use strategically to solve a series of chemical engineering challenges within a fixegived time. From constructing process flows and solving engineering problems to making critical decisions about when to use hints or conserve resources, every round will test the participants’ ability to think efficiently under constraints. The event aims to create an engaging and competitive environment where participants are rewarded not only for what they know, but also for how effectively they apply their knowledge, manage limited resources, and make decisions under pressure.
