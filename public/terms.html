Accounts and registration

  • You sign in with your own Google account. One account can hold one registration.
  • Your details must be true and must match your college ID card. You upload a photo of its front when you register, and show the card itself at the gate.
  • You are responsible for keeping your account secure and for activity under it.

Fee and payment

  • The registration fee is ₹999, paid by UPI to the account shown on the registration form.
  • After paying, you upload a screenshot of the payment and enter its UPI transaction ID (UTR).
  • Your registration stays pending until the registration team has verified the payment. It is then confirmed or rejected.
  • A registration is rejected if the payment can't be verified, for example a wrong amount or an unclear or edited screenshot. You can then register again with a valid payment.
  • A UTR can be used for only one registration. We may block accounts that submit false payment details.

Refunds

The registration fee is non-refundable once your registration is confirmed. If you were charged twice, or your payment reached us but your registration was rejected, contact us with your UTR and we will look into a refund. If the fest is cancelled, we will announce how fees will be refunded.

Entry pass

  • A confirmed registration gets one QR entry pass, shown under MY PASS on the site.
  • The pass is personal and non-transferable. Don't share it: whoever is scanned first is checked in.
  • Each pass checks in once. Carry your college ID; volunteers may refuse entry if it doesn't match your registration.
