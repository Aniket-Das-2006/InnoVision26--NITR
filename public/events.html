Innovision 2026 Events

Hosted by NIT Rourkela, Odisha, Innovision is Eastern India's largest techno-management fest. Its 2026 edition, The Celestial Odyssey, charts four worlds: Flagship Events for the technical arena, Standout Events for the signature showcases, Main Events for workshops and talks, and DTS and Fun Events for the games, quizzes and showcases where the fest peaks.
