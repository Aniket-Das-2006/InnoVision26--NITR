INNOVISION

Innovision is NIT Rourkela's techno-management fest and the largest in Eastern India: a few days where the campus turns into a launch pad for builders, thinkers and makers from across the country.

Pick your world. Flagship Events for the technical arena, Standout Events for the signature showcases, Main Events for workshops and talks, or DTS and Fun Events for the games, quizzes and showcases where the fest peaks.
